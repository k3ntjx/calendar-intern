Privacy policy

Calendar Intern is a private Telegram bot used only by its owner to manage the owner's own Google Calendar.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, it reads and edits events on the owner's Google calendars, and reads the list of those calendars.

How the data is used

Calendar data is used only to show the owner their events, send them reminders, and make the calendar changes they ask for. Messages and images the owner sends to the bot are processed to understand event details. That processing uses Anthropic's Claude API.

Sharing and storage

The data is not sold, shared with other people, or used for advertising. The bot keeps no copy of calendar events beyond what is needed to answer a request.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
